# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring teams evaluating Eleazar Hernandez for software-engineering roles and freelance clients evaluating a software partner. Both audiences need to understand the breadth of work he can take on and his ability to turn ambiguous needs into working software.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Eleazar Hernandez’s work, professional background, writing, services, and contact path. Its success is a qualified conversation with a hiring team or freelance client.

## Positioning

Eleazar is a broadly capable, hands-on software engineer: a one-stop partner for varied software use cases and scenarios who builds independently to gain a deeper understanding of the work.

## Operating Context

Visitors review featured projects, professional experience and skills, Substack posts, freelance services, a résumé, and contact options. The repository includes portfolio, contact, and notes routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing application is a Next.js/React portfolio with a contact form, Substack-feed integration, and email sending via Resend. Product-specific accessibility requirements and additional non-negotiable constraints are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Eleazar Hernandez and the stated role “AI-Native Software Engineer” where applicable. Preserve factual professional details and project claims; do not invent credentials, clients, outcomes, or evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository assets include a headshot (`portfolio-site-next/public/headshot.png`), résumé (`portfolio-site-next/public/Eleazar_Hernandez_Resume.pdf`), and the Rotate Translation project image (`portfolio-site-next/public/gavel_for_rotate_translation.png`). The code contains professional-history, skills, project, service, and social-link copy. No independently confirmed testimonials, metrics, client list, or other proof is on hand.

## Product Principles

- Make breadth of software capability understandable without overstating it.
- Show concrete work and factual experience before asking for contact.
- Treat curiosity and independent learning as evidence of practical problem-solving.
- Support both employment and freelance evaluation paths.
- Preserve trust through accurate, supportable claims.
